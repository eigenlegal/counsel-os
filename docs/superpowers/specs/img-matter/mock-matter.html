Context

New, standalone revenue-share agreement (not the standard Content License & Distribution Agreement, though it reuses its revenue mechanics, audit rights, and platform-security machinery). Sinai builds and wholly owns a separate K-12 platform; Lerner contributes ~3,000 children's books and its school-district sales force. Signing expected July 2026; mutual 6-month launch target; binding Technical & Target Customer Addendum due within 30 days of signing.

Track: RED

Structural concessions client-approved on the 2026-06-25 call.

Deal terms as scoped

Structure: contractual partnership, no JV or equity. Separate K-12 platform fully owned by the Sinai corporate entity — Lerner owns nothing in the platform, technology, or brand.

Economics: pure revenue share, per-seat license model. Schools wire Sinai directly. Bracketed Technology Cost Deduction capped at 10% of revenue.
