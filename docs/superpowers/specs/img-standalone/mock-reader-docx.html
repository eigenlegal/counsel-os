2.Obligations

2.1Each Receiving Party shall hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party except to its employees, officers, directors, and professional advisers who have a need to know and are bound by written obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out here.

2.2The Receiving Party shall protect the Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

R. Patel · Aug 28Standard of care: we can't commit to "same degree" — our internal policy is stricter than what we'd accept contractually. Reasonable care is market.

2.3Confidential Information may be used solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose.

3.Term

3.1This Agreement governs disclosures made during the two (2) yearone (1) year period beginning on the Effective Date. The obligations in Section 2 survive for five (5) yearstwo (2) years after the date of each disclosure, and with respect to trade secrets, for so long as such information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

6.Residuals

6.1Nothing in this Agreement restricts either party from using Residual Knowledge. "Residual Knowledge" means information retained in the unaided memory of persons who had access to Confidential Information, including ideas, concepts, know-how, and techniques.
